## Action summary

On 2024-12-19 OFAC:
1. Sanctioned **12 individuals and entities** tied to Houthi arms trafficking, illicit shipping, money laundering
2. **Expanded designation of Sa'id al-Jamal** — key IRGC-QF aligned Houthi financier
3. Added **5 cryptocurrency addresses** to al-Jamal's SDN entry

### Scale
- al-Jamal's addresses received $178M+ over ~1 year
- 1700+ USDT transfers, avg ~$638K
- Largest single outbound: $6.8M on 2024-10-07

## Circle response

On 2024-12-20, Circle blacklisted 5 unique addresses across 6 EVM chains = 30 total BLACKLIST actions.
