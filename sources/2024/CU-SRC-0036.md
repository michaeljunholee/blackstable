## Action summary

On 2024-03-27, OFAC + UK OFSI jointly sanctioned 2 individuals + 3 entities for raising money for Hamas after the 2023-10-07 attacks on Israel.

### Designated parties
- **Gaza Now** — Gaza-based social media news outlet (Telegram); posted pro-Hamas content, solicited crypto donations
- **Mustafa Ayash** — Founder/director of Gaza Now
- **Al-Qureshi Executives** and **Aakhirah Limited** — London-based businesses sending money to Gaza Now
- **Aozma Sultana** — Director of Al-Qureshi Executives / Aakhirah Ltd

### Crypto addresses
- 1 empty BTC address
- 2 ETH addresses holding >$2,690
- 5 USDT addresses holding >$10,350

Total raised via crypto: ~$4.5M

### Historical significance
- **First-ever joint OFAC + UK OFSI crypto designation**
- **First-ever UK OFSI inclusion of crypto addresses in its consolidated financial sanctions list**

## Circle response

On 2024-03-27, Circle blacklisted 4 addresses × 6 EVM chains = 24 total BLACKLIST actions. 2 are the directly-attested ETH addresses plus 2 additional addresses linked to the network.
