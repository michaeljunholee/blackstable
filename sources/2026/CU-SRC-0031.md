## Action summary

On 2026-01-30 OFAC sanctioned:
1. **Zedcex** and **Zedxion** — UK-registered front companies (IRGC-linked; processed tens of billions in Iran-aligned transactions)
2. **Babak Zanjani** — notorious Iranian financier, long tied to regime corruption and sanctions evasion
3. **6 high-volume wallet addresses** associated with the exchanges' operational infrastructure

This is the **first-ever** OFAC designation of an IRGC-linked digital asset exchange.
