## Action summary

On 2025-11-19 OFAC designated (in coordination with Australia and UK):

### Cybercrime infrastructure
- **Media Land** — Russia-based bulletproof hosting (BPH) provider supporting ransomware operations
- **3 Media Land leadership members**
- **3 sister companies** including **ML Cloud**
- **Hypercore Ltd.** — alleged front company of previously-designated Aeza Group LLC (another BPH provider)
- **Aleksandr Aleksandrovich Volosovik** — aka "Ohyeahhellno," "podzemniy1," "Yalishanda" (St. Petersburg); XBT address `18dLDAWi8LmrHbEq3QzDJb9SLxCf4uimXB`

### Drug trafficking
- **Ryan James Wedding** — former Canadian Olympic snowboarder, FBI Ten Most Wanted
- **9 individuals and 9 entities** linked to Wedding's trafficking network

## Circle response

On 2025-11-19, Circle blacklisted ~5-7 addresses across 6 EVM chains = 32 total BLACKLIST actions.
