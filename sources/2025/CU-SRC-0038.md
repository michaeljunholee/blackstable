## Action summary

On 2025-03-21, OFAC officially delisted Tornado Cash from the Specially Designated Nationals (SDN) List, reversing the August 8, 2022 designation.

### Scope of removal
- Over 100 Ethereum addresses removed from SDN
- Tornado Cash entity itself delisted
- Treasury acknowledged "novel legal and policy issues" raised by the Fifth Circuit ruling

### Legal basis for delisting
Following November 2024 U.S. Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals ruling (Van Loon v. Treasury) that OFAC exceeded authority by sanctioning Tornado Cash's immutable smart contracts, which do not qualify as "property" under IEEPA. OFAC exercised discretion to lift sanctions.

## Circle response

Circle reversed its earlier Tornado Cash blacklists across multiple chains:
- **2025-03-21**: 47 UNBLACKLIST actions (initial ethereum cleanup)
- **2025-03-22**: 493 UNBLACKLIST actions (cross-chain propagation to avalanche, arbitrum, base, optimism, polygon)

Total: 540 UNBLACKLIST events reversing the 2022-08-08/09 Tornado Cash blacklist campaign.
