## Action summary

On 2025-04-02 OFAC sanctioned a network of financial and logistical facilitators tied to the Iran-backed Houthis (Ansarallah), including:
- Russia-based actors and shipping companies
- Facilitators of arms, stolen Ukrainian grain, and illicit payments
- 8 cryptocurrency wallet addresses: 6 private wallets + 2 deposit addresses at mainstream services; together moved nearly $1B
- Houthi-linked addresses using OFAC SDN Garantex as deposit ($45M+ via Garantex)
- Wallets also designated by Israel's National Bureau for Counter Terror Financing (NBCTF)
