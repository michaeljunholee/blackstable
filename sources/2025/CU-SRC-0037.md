## Action summary

On 2025-02-11 OFAC + FCDO + DFAT coordinated sanctions against **Zservers**, a Russia-based bulletproof hosting provider (BPH) for LockBit ransomware.

### Designated parties
- **Zservers** (Russia-based BPH provider) — 3 crypto addresses
- **Aleksandr Sergeyeevich Bolshakov** — Zservers administrator
- **Alexander Igorevich Mishin** — Zservers administrator — 1 crypto address

### Financial scope
- Chainalysis: $5.2M+ on-chain activity
- 200+ crypto accounts frozen by authorities
- Connections to Garantex and no-KYC services

## Circle response

On 2025-02-24 (13 days after OFAC designation), Circle blacklisted 3 unique addresses × 6 EVM chains = 18 total BLACKLIST actions. The delay likely reflects either a Circle-internal review of Zservers-associated addresses beyond the 4 directly attested in the OFAC SDN entry, or OFAC-coordinated secondary address disclosure.

## Attribution confidence

MEDIUM — timing offset of 13 days from OFAC designation is non-standard for Circle's typical same-day compliance pattern. Possible alternative attribution: a follow-on Feb 24 2025 OFAC action adding more addresses to the Zservers entry, or a distinct incident.
