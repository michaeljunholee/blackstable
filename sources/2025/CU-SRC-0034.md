## Action summary

Historic US-UK coordinated action on 2025-10-14 against Prince Group Transnational Criminal Organization (Prince Group TCO), a Cambodia-based scam compound operator:

### Criminal indictment (DOJ, Brooklyn federal court)
- **Chen Zhi ("Vincent")** — Prince Group founder, charged with wire fraud conspiracy and money laundering conspiracy
- Charges relate to forced-labor scam compounds across Cambodia running "pig butchering" cryptocurrency investment fraud

### DOJ civil forfeiture
- **127,271 Bitcoin seized** (~$14.4B at time of action) — **largest crypto forfeiture in DOJ history**
- 4 Bitcoin addresses directly associated with Chen Zhi (holding ~$1.8B)

### OFAC designation
- **Prince Group** designated as Transnational Criminal Organization
- **146 entities and individuals** added to SDN List — largest OFAC action against Southeast Asian cybercrime

### FinCEN section 311 final rule
- **Huione Group** — severed from US financial system; critical money-laundering node for pig-butchering proceeds

### UK FCDO simultaneous action
- UK Foreign Commonwealth Development Office imposed coordinated sanctions

## Circle response

On 2025-10-14, Circle blacklisted multi-chain addresses: 4 unique addrs × 6 chains + 7 extra on polygon + 4 extra on ethereum = 31 total BLACKLIST actions. These are Circle's matching of the Prince Group SDN additions with on-chain EVM counterparts.

## Scale / Historical significance

- **$15B in total crypto seized** (including wraparounds with other seizures)
- Multi-jurisdiction coordination (US + UK + later joined by South Korea)
- Represents OFAC's recognition that pig-butchering scams are now a top-tier transnational criminal enforcement priority
