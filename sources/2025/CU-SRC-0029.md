## Action summary

On 2025-08-14 OFAC:
1. **Re-designated Garantex Europe OU** — Russian cryptocurrency exchange that processed $100M+ in ransomware / cybercrime transactions since 2019. Original designation was Apr 5, 2022; the Mar 6, 2025 Secret Service takedown seized $26M and disrupted infrastructure, but Garantex officers re-emerged as Grinex.
2. **Designated Grinex** — Garantex's successor exchange created after the March 2025 takedown to continue sanctions-evasion activity. Grinex openly marketed itself as a sanctions-evasion vehicle and facilitated $1B+ in transactions through mid-2025.
3. **Three Garantex executives** sanctioned as individuals.
4. **Six associated companies** in Russia and Kyrgyz Republic added to SDN.
5. **A7A5 ruble-backed token network** — A7A5 is a Russian ruble-pegged token deployed on Tron and Ethereum that had processed $51B+ in volume through July 2025.
6. **15+ crypto wallet addresses** added across ETH, ARB, BSC, Tron.

## Circle response (2025-08-14)

On 2025-08-14 Circle blacklisted 12 additional addresses across 6 EVM chains (eth 14, base 11, polygon 11, avalanche 11, arbitrum 11, optimism 11 — 72 total replication + 39 pre-existing triggered actions = 111 events). The 12 new addresses correspond to the new Garantex/Grinex ecosystem additions announced August 14.
